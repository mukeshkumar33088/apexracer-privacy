Privacy Policy — Apex Racer: Driver Career Sim

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Android app com.mobile.apexracer, developed by Mukesh Kumar.

The game runs on your device and has no account or sign-in. We do not collect or store your personal data. The game offers optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. Players who own the VIP Pack never see ads.

The app talks to the internet only to load and show those optional ads, and when you choose to make an in-app purchase, which is handled by Google Play.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app saves is written to its own private storage on your device and never leaves it:

  • Your careers: up to three saved drivers, with their results, seasons, finances, possessions and history.
  • Your settings, such as whether vibration is on.
  • Your purchases: which one-time items you own (for example a Bigger Tank pack), so their effect applies to every career.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, and no way for us to recover it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos. The driver name you type is stored only on your device.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry of our own.
  • It does not send your saved games or any other content to us or to anyone else.
  • It does not use cookies or any similar tracking technology.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Each game week the end-of-week screen offers a few free rewards (extra energy, a Hype Boost, Double Cash). You get one by choosing to watch a short video ad. Ads are never shown unless you tap one of these rewards.

The ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with the ads, and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the ads library.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and you can play without agreeing. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you own the VIP Pack, the game does not start the ads library at all and the weekly rewards are given without an ad.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases: energy, in-game cash, permanent max-energy upgrades and a one-time VIP Pack (no ads, a VIP badge, cash and max energy). None of them is needed to play.

All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your payment details: no card number, no billing address, nothing. Google tells the app only which item was bought. Google Play's own handling of your data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets Google Play Billing process purchases.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used by Google Play Billing and by Google AdMob to load the optional ads. No other part of the game uses the network.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: lets AdMob read the advertising ID, as described above.
  • VIBRATE: short vibrations on button presses, at the start lights and during qualifying. You can turn them off in the game's settings.

Bug reports

The game's settings have a Report a bug button. It opens your own email app with a message addressed to us and a few lines filled in (the game version, your Android version, and the week and series of your career). Nothing is sent unless you press send yourself. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote, and use them only to reply and fix the problem. We do not share them or add you to any mailing list.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It is a motor-racing career simulation written for teens and adults. We do not collect personal data ourselves; ads are served by AdMob as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the date at the top will change.
